## Information processed when you visit

We serve this website from a virtual private server (VPS) that we administer, using Cloudflare in front of the site to deliver content and protect it from malicious traffic. Our VPS infrastructure provider is Hetzner, and the server is located in United States/Ashburn, VA.

When your browser requests a page or file, our hosting infrastructure and Cloudflare process technical information needed to handle the request. Depending on the service and logging settings, this can include your IP address, requested URL, date and time, browser and operating-system information, referring page, response status, and security or error information. Our origin server may record Cloudflare's IP address rather than yours, depending on its configuration.

We use this information to deliver the website, maintain availability, investigate errors, and detect or prevent abuse.

The website does not require an account. Documentation search runs in your browser; the site's search feature does not send your search terms to a search service.

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your contact details and message. We use it to respond and handle your request. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

## Browser storage

The website uses browser storage for the following features:

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lightning-theme` | Local storage | Remembers the light or dark theme you select. | No automatic expiry; remains until cleared by you or your browser. |
| `lightning-docs-text-size` | Local storage | Remembers the documentation text size you select. | No automatic expiry; remains until cleared by you or your browser. |
| `lightning-intro-seen` | Session storage | Remembers that the homepage introduction has been shown so it does not repeat during the same page session. | Normally until the tab or window is closed; browser session restoration may preserve it. |

These values are used by the site's interface and are not sent to our server by the website's application code. You can clear them using your browser's site-data controls. Blocking storage may prevent preferences from being remembered. Changing a preference updates its stored value.

## Google Fonts

The website currently loads Manrope and Cormorant Garamond from Google Fonts. This means your browser connects to Google when loading the font stylesheet and font files. Google receives your IP address and request information, such as browser headers, as part of those connections.

Google states that its Fonts API does not set or log cookies and does not use information collected through the service to create profiles or target advertising. See [Google's explanation of Fonts privacy](https://fonts.googleblog.com/2022/11/your-privacy-and-google-fonts.html).
